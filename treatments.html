Specialized Care Programmes

Lifestyle care

Life Style Disease Management Program

For diabetes, hypertension, and hypercholesterolemia management. This program may include Shirodhara, Nasyam, Udwarthanam, and Vasthi according to the severity of the problem. The course of treatment will be 17–35 days.

Our program takes a holistic approach to disease management, addressing not just the symptoms but also the underlying imbalances contributing to hypercholesterolemia.

Mind & wellbeing

Stress & Anxiety Management Programme

This program may comprise Shirodhara, Nasyam, Vasti, and Snehapanam, along with meditation and Pranayama. According to the severity of the problem, the course of treatment will be 10–25 days.

Weight management

Slimming / Anti-Obesity Program

This program may comprise Shirodhara, Udwarthanam (powder massage), Vasti, Snehapanam, Vamanam, and Virechanam. According to the body mass index, the course of treatment will be 17–35 days.

Our Ayurvedic Slimming / Anti-Obesity Program is designed to guide you on a holistic journey towards sustainable weight management and overall well-being.

Spine & mobility

Ayurveda for Back Pain, Spinal Disorders and Deformities

Problems often seen among IT professionals include cervical and lumbar spondylitis, insomnia, and other stress-related concerns.

This program may comprise Shirodhara, Nasyam, Kizhi, Kateetharpanam, and Vasti. According to the severity of the problem, the course of treatment will be 17–35 days.

Ayurvedic treatment for spinal disorders and deformities focuses on maintaining the normal curvature of the spine and relieving nerve compression. External therapies such as Abhyanga and Vasthi, along with specific medication, may help strengthen muscles and alleviate pain. Panchakarma is used to support the body's cleansing process and help keep the spine relaxed.
