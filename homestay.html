Your room and shared spaces

Comfortable private rooms in Poovar

Gokulam Stay Inn offers four independent, air-conditioned private rooms. Each room has a comfortable bed and an attached private bathroom. Rooms are offered separately, on a per-room basis; please enquire about availability and pricing before booking.

Four private rooms

Each independent room is air-conditioned and arranged for a comfortable, relaxing stay.

Private bathrooms

Every room has its own attached private bathroom for convenience and privacy.

Shared kitchen and dining

Guests can make use of the shared kitchen and dining area, along with spacious common areas.

Peaceful surroundings

Stay close to the scenic Poovar Backwaters and Golden Sand Beach, with nature and local attractions nearby.

For families and couples

A comfortable base for families, couples and travellers looking to enjoy a quiet stay close to nature.

Wellness on site

Ayurhitham Wellness Centre is located on the same premises. Consultations and treatments are available separately by appointment.

Non-smoking and alcohol-free

Gokulam Stay Inn is a non-smoking and alcohol-free property.

Discover Poovar

Backwaters, beach and village life

Gokulam Stay Inn is around 100 metres from Poovar Backwaters and close to Golden Sand Beach. The surrounding coastal neighbourhood offers a quieter base for enjoying the waterways, greenery and local sights.

Trivandrum International Airport is about 31 km away.
