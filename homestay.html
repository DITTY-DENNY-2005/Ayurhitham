Coastal setting

Enjoy the peaceful village surroundings, with a lake view and beach access listed as amenities.

For couples and families

The listing describes the stay as suitable for couples and families looking for a calm Poovar getaway.

Ayurveda on site

Ayurhitham Ayurveda Wellness Centre is on the premises. Consultations and treatments are available separately by appointment.

Discover Poovar

Backwaters, beach and village life

Gokulam Stay Inn is around 100 metres from Poovar Backwaters and close to Golden Sand Beach. The surrounding coastal neighbourhood offers a quieter base for enjoying the waterways, greenery and local sights.

Trivandrum International Airport is about 31 km away, according to the listing.
