She Clinic

Menopause-Related Symptom Management

Menopause is a natural stage in a woman's life that may bring physical, hormonal and emotional changes. At Ayurhitham Wellness, we offer personalised Ayurvedic care and supportive wellness programs to help women manage menopause-related symptoms and improve their quality of life.

Our approach focuses on individual health assessment, dietary guidance, lifestyle modifications, stress management and appropriate Ayurvedic therapies based on the physician's recommendation.

Our goal is to support women through this transition with comfort, confidence and holistic care.

Vaginal Health & Discomfort Management

Vaginal discomfort, dryness, itching, irritation and other intimate health concerns can affect a woman's comfort and quality of life. These symptoms may become more common during menopause due to hormonal changes.

At Ayurhitham, we provide personalised consultations and supportive care to address women's intimate health concerns, including menopause-related vaginal dryness and discomfort.

Our approach includes individual health assessment, appropriate medical investigations when required, lifestyle and dietary guidance, and physician-recommended treatment.

We aim to promote intimate health, comfort and overall women's wellness through a holistic and patient-centred approach.

  • Menopause-Related Vaginal Discomfort
