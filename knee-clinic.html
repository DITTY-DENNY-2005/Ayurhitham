Knee Clinic

We provide expert care for all knee conditions—acute injuries, chronic pain, and post-surgery rehabilitation—by combining advanced medical treatments with holistic Ayurvedic therapies. Our approach integrates diagnostics and physiotherapy with herbal oil massages (Abhyanga), therapeutic poultices (Patra Pinda Sweda), anti-inflammatory herbal formulations, and yoga-based exercises.

This integrative care not only relieves pain but also restores mobility, strengthens joints, and promotes long-term knee health. Whether recovering from surgery, healing from an injury, managing arthritis, or seeking preventive care, the combination of Ayurvedic therapies and physiotherapy can accelerate recovery, reduce pain and swelling, and restore movement. Our personalized programs help you regain strength, flexibility, and confidence with every step.
