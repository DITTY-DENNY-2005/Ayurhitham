Adolescent Training

We provide guidance and counseling on menstrual care, hygiene, self-confidence, and sex education for teen girls. Our sessions create a safe space where teenagers can openly discuss their concerns and challenges, including body image, peer pressure, emotional stress, mood swings, academic pressure, nutrition, and developing healthy habits. Through counseling, awareness, and education, teens gain confidence, understand their bodies, and learn effective coping mechanisms.
