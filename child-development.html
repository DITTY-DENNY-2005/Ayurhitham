Child Development

Assessment & Treatment for Differently Talented Children

Our support includes

  • Counseling & Emotional Support – building confidence and resilience
  • Speech Therapy – improving communication skills
  • Occupational Therapy – enhancing daily living and motor skills
  • Behavioral Therapy – managing behavior and attention issues
  • Physiotherapy – strengthening movement and coordination
  • Autism Support – tailored programs for children on the spectrum
  • ADHD Management – focus and learning strategies
  • Learning Disability Support – personalized teaching methods
  • Educational Guidance – customized learning plans

Helping every child discover their potential with care and compassion.
