Chief Medical Officer & Advisor

Dr. Gokula G

BAMS, MHSc CCD

Dr. Gokula G is a seasoned Ayurvedic physician with over a decade of experience in traditional medicine and clinical child development. She earned her Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) in 2008 from Bapuji Ayurveda Medical College, affiliated with Rajeev Gandhi University of Health Sciences. Following her internship, she served as a Medical Officer at Nanthavanam Ayurveda in Thrissur, Kerala, for three years, before continuing her practice at Vivekananda Co-Operative Society Hospital in Trivandrum.

In 2015, Dr. Gokula pursued a Postgraduate Degree in Clinical Child Development (MHSc CCD) from the Child Development Centre at Trivandrum Medical College, Kerala University. During her tenure, she specialized in the assessment and therapy of children with disabilities, including autism spectrum disorder, cerebral palsy, and hyperactivity disorders and more.

Dr. Gokula's international experience includes three years at New Life Traditional Ayurveda Clinic in Sohar, Sultanate of Oman, followed by a position at Lamasat Ayurveda Clinic in Muscat. Currently, she serves as the Chief Medical Officer and Advisor at Ayurhitham Ayurveda.

Specializing in traditional Ayurvedic therapies and clinical child development, Dr. Gokula provides personalized holistic care, blending ancient practices with modern healthcare principles to promote overall wellness, balance, and harmony. Driven by a deep passion for helping others, she is committed to guiding patients toward a healthier, balanced life, ensuring the best possible outcomes for their well-being.

“May God bless us all, giving us the strength and guidance to heal and take care of each other!”
